InvoiceFlow Store

Point-of-sale and invoicing software for shops in Pakistan.

InvoiceFlow Store is a Windows desktop app for general, medical and electrical stores. It handles sales at the counter, invoices, stock, purchases and reports. It works offline: your business data is kept on your own PC.

The app can keep an encrypted copy of your nightly backup in your own Google Drive. It only ever sees the backup files it created itself, never anything else in your Drive.
