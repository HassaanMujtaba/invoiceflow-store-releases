Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

This policy covers the InvoiceFlow Store desktop app ("the app") and the InvoiceFlow licence server.

Your business data stays on your PC

Products, customers, sales, invoices, stock, staff and every other record you enter are stored in a database on your own computer. We do not receive, see or store them.

What the licence server receives

To check that a licence is valid, the app contacts our licence server when it is activated and then regularly to renew. It sends:

  • the licence key, and an ID for this installation;
  • scrambled (hashed) hardware identifiers, so a licence can't be copied to another PC. The real identifiers are never sent;
  • the PC's name, Windows version and app version;
  • two numbers: how many users are active, and how many invoices were made in the last 30 days. No invoice contents are sent;
  • the internet address the request came from.

We also keep the business name, city, phone number and email address that you or your reseller gave when the licence was issued. We use all of this only to run, support and protect licensing. We don't sell it or share it with advertisers.

Google Drive backups (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, the app uploads your nightly backup to your own Drive account. Backups are encrypted on your PC with your backup passphrase before they are uploaded, so neither Google nor we can read them.

The app asks Google only for the drive.file permission. That lets it see and manage the backup files it created itself, and nothing else in your Drive. The Google sign-in token is stored on your PC only and never reaches our servers. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect Drive in the app at any time, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backup files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

No tracking

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

How long we keep it

Licence records are kept while the licence is in use and for up to 2 years after, for support and billing. Ask us to delete them earlier and we will, unless we have to keep them by law.
